Connections

Connections are optional. Copy for Docs, Word and HTML files work without them. Connect Jira or Figma if you want to send comments straight there. You sign in once on this computer.

  • Nothing is sent until you choose Send to Jira or Send to Figma in the report, and you see what goes before it goes.
  • Margin asks Chrome for access to a service only when you click Connect.
  • Disconnect signs you out and removes that access.
