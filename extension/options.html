Help and settings

Margin puts a sheet over the page you are reviewing. Mark what looks wrong, number the details, and hand over the review as a Doc, a Word file or a web page.

Getting started

The welcome tour walks through a full review in six steps. The tips point at the real buttons the first time you open the popup and the report.

While annotating

Drag
Mark an area. A note opens beside it.
Click
Pick the element under the pointer, when selector capture is on.
S
Turn selector capture on or off.
Click in the box
Drop a numbered marker inside the region you just marked. Right-click one to remove it.
M
Turn numbered markers on or off.
Right-click
Comment on one element or the whole visible area, switch selectors, or stop.
Ctrl Enter
Save the note.
Esc
Close the menu or the note, then stop annotating.

In the image editor

Open it from the report by clicking any screenshot, or with Edit image in Edit comments.

V M B A
Select, Marker, Box and Arrow.
Drag a handle
Resize a box, an arrow or the marked region.
Right-click a number
Remove that item. The rest renumber.
Right-click elsewhere
Add, duplicate, renumber, reset the region or change the zoom.
Double-click
Write the item's note.
Ctrl Z
Undo. Add Shift to redo.
Delete
Remove the selected item.
Arrow keys
Nudge the selection by one pixel, or ten with Shift.
+ - 0 1
Zoom in, zoom out, fit and actual size.

Chrome's right-click menu

Right-click any page to start or stop annotating, comment on the whole visible area, or open the review report.
